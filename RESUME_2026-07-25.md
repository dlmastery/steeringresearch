## 2. The three things that must not be forgotten

### a) yoganext's "agent-first" claim is NOT earned yet
A UI audit found **10 capabilities reachable by tap but not by chat**. The original
`verifyToolCoverage()` could never have caught it — it compares the contract to its own
implementations, never to the UI. Reporting "14/14" was a check validating the wrong thing.

**Definition of done:** `npm run verify:agent` passes AND has been *demonstrated to fail*
when a UI→tool mapping is removed. A check never observed failing is decoration.

### b) The voice SOTA goal has a two-bar standard
On SVD, **age alone = ROC-AUC 0.871** (F1, `FINDINGS.md`) vs published **UAR 85.22**.
So "beating SOTA" is achievable by exploiting the demographic confound. Every dataset must
report **four numbers**: published SOTA · ours · confound baseline · margin. A win that
does not clear the confound bar is logged **NOT CLEARED**, not announced.

Current decoded pilot is **49 speakers** — too small to claim a benchmark. Wait for the full corpus.

### c) Steering is blocked on judge-dependent claims
Two calibrations failed: AUC **0.665** (integer readout) → **0.7508** (continuous, +0.086,
12.8× faster) — both under the 0.85 gate. See `autoresearch_results/JUDGE_CARD.md`.
Program is re-scoped to **judge-free endpoints** (probe AUC vs ground truth, WikiText
perplexity, geometry). Do not shop for a third judge before doing science.
